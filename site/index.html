GOOD TO KNOW

Questions

What do I actually get when I buy?

A license key plus downloads for your platform — an Android APK and a Windows installer (or portable build). One purchase covers both.

Which devices are supported?

Android phones and tablets, Windows 10/11 (64-bit), and any modern browser for the Live edition.

Do updates cost extra?

No. Every edition auto-updates for free, and rolls back on its own if an update ever fails to start.

Can I use my MIDI keyboard?

Yes — USB and Bluetooth LE controllers both work, with MIDI-learn to map any pad or key. Paid editions only.

How do refunds work?

Reach out through the support link in the footer and we'll sort it out.
